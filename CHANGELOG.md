# Changelog

## 2.5.0
- X site theme (x.com and twitter.com), for the Lights out and Dim themes. The light theme and custom accent colours other than blue are left as they are.

## 2.4.0
- Reddit site theme. Vote arrows keep their colours.

## 2.3.0
- Instagram site theme, pop-ups included.
- Every `--ambxst-*` colour also comes as an `R, G, B` triplet, `--ambxst-*-rgb`.

## 2.2.1
- DuckDuckGo home page (duckduckgo.com and start.duckduckgo.com).

## 2.2.0
- DuckDuckGo site theme.
- Web page colours are switched in Zen: two checkboxes in the Ambxst mod's settings, applied without restart. The Ambxst-side Web pages setting is gone. Rerun install.sh and restart Zen.

## 2.1.0
- Web pages: selection, scrollbars and form controls on every site, and styles for YouTube, GitHub, Wikipedia and Twitch. Rerun install.sh and restart Zen to get them.

## 2.0.0
- Creates the "Ambxst" mod in Zen itself and finds the Zen profiles on its own.
- `install.sh` finds the Zen installs on its own.

## 1.1.0
- Optional Zen startup script: updates open windows without opening one.

## 1.0.0
- First version.
